Attached is a paper that I showed at yesterday’s Clinical Trial Think Tank.

The issue was whether the Weibull distribution is a reasonable model for cancer survival data.  My claim is that it is not (unless it is also exponentially distributed).  Figure 2 shows a 2-parameter generalizedgamma modeling graph shown in the Christopher Cox et al paper (also attached).  Our figure 2 is a modification of their figure 1, with modeling results of SEER data for four different cancers at three different stages.  The data show that they are closestto the ammag distribution fit (the curvy descending line, given the name “gamma” spelled backwards due to its reciprocal relationship to a gamma distribution, which  the straight line with a slope of 1)  or to the lognormal fit (the dot-dash line with shapeparameter = 0).  The Weibull model, with shape parameter = 1 is not particularly close to the data (which an overlaid plot would dramatically show).
